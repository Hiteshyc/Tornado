Coastal Hazard Prevention Application
- Uses AI model to predict the hazardous coastal areas well in advance providing time for evacuation.
- Designed for operational teams to easily perform the evacuation duties.
- 3 types of access will be granted:
    1. Admin(Reserved by Dev team , organization dept.) -> features provided will be monitor, control.
    2. Officer(Operational Rescue Teams) -> features provided will be monitor, control(limited access).
    3. User(Normal citizens)->only monitor,report.

Functionalities:
--> Analysis Dashboard with Real-time Hazard Map, Active Alerts(with their location,degree of danger,etc), Real-timne data.
--> Emergency Contact Information(call , messages)
--> Reporting of hazardous incidents
--> Alerting Dashboard with detailed insight on all the active alerts.

Role of AI:
1. Train ML model to predict the hazardous incidents with the help of satellite data.
2. Use the model for predicting hazards and alerting in advance.
3. Maintain a trust ratio that grows as per accuracy automatically.
